How it works

STEP 1

Pick a piece

361 exercises in three quests: timeless melodies like Für Elise, Greensleeves and Danny Boy, the classical studies of Fernando Sor, and a step-by-step curriculum of scales, chords, arpeggios, fingerpicking, rhythm and lead.

STEP 2

Play into the microphone

No cable, no setup. Play your acoustic or classical guitar near your phone or tablet. The score scrolls with you, and each note turns green the moment TabRider hears it.

STEP 3

Earn stars, unlock more

Every run is scored on the right note, the timing and how well you hold it. Stars unlock the next row of pieces, so there is always something new to play.

Everything you need to practise

Tab or standard notation

Read tablature or sheet music, and switch at any time.

Slow it down

Practice runs are never scored. Drop the tempo to 25 %, or let note by note wait for you on every note.

A built-in tuner

Tune each string in seconds before you play.

  • Your own music. Import Guitar Pro 6 files (.gpx) into My Library.
  • Metronome. A count-in and a steady click keep you on the beat.
  • Private. Sound is analysed on your device, never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Free. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and it works offline.
