Privacy Policy TabRider · Last updated: May 30, 2026 TabRider ("the app", "we") is a guitar-learning application for iOS. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled. Microphone access TabRider uses your device's microphone to listen to the notes you play on your guitar. Audio is analysed entirely on your device in real time using Apple's Accelerate framework (FFT / harmonic analysis). No audio data — raw or processed — is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device. Data we collect TabRider does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Specifically: No account or login is required. No audio recordings are made or retained. No usage analytics or crash reports are sent to any server. No advertising identifiers are accessed. No data is shared with third parties. Locally stored data The app stores your exercise scores and progression state locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly back it up via iCloud (subject to your own iCloud settings — TabRider does not configure or initiate iCloud sync). If you import your own Guitar Pro (.gpx) files, those files are stored in the app's private Documents folder on your device and are not transmitted anywhere. Children's privacy TabRider does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Changes to this policy If the app's data practices change in a future version (for example, if cloud sync or online features are added), this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
